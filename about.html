About

ContestRadar tracks upcoming contests across Codeforces, LeetCode, CodeChef and AtCoder in a single schedule - then goes further: link your handles and it follows your ratings, charts your progress, and keeps a personal watchlist of the contests you care about.

What it does

  • Contests - Merges upcoming schedule from all four platforms, with live badges, countdowns, search, platform filters and rating-based eligibility on Codeforces contests.
  • Analytics - Per-platform rating graphs with hover details, current / best / worst / average deltas, participation streaks, tier placement, contest history and insights.
  • Profile - Your handles, connected-rating summary, and cross-device sync when signed in.

Where the data comes from

  • Codeforces - official public API.
  • LeetCode, CodeChef contests - community CompeteAPI mirror.
  • AtCoder contests - community Contest Hive mirror.
  • LeetCode ratings - community alfa API; AtCoder history - public history feed; CodeChef history - a purpose-built Cloudflare Worker that reads public profile data.

Community feeds can be flaky, so the app caches aggressively and degrades gracefully - something missing usually means a mirror is down, not your account.

How it's built

A static frontend (no frameworks, no build step) with Google sign-in and cloud sync on Firebase, plus the Cloudflare Worker above. Your data lives under per-user security rules - nobody can read anyone else's profile.
