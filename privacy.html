Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5th October 2026.ContestRadar collects the bare minimum data needed to run, never sells data and loads no third-party trackers.

What we collect

  • Google account (only if you sign in): name and email address, provided by Google Sign-In.
  • Platform handles: the Codeforces, LeetCode, AtCoder and CodeChef usernames you enter, plus when each was last changed (this powers the 30-day handle-change cooldown).
  • App data: tracked contests, participation ticks, theme choice, and cached rating histories.

How it's used

  • Personalizing the contest schedule, analytics and reminders.
  • Syncing your profile across your devices when signed in.
  • Nothing else. No profiling, no advertising, no resale - ever.

Where it lives

  • Signed-in data is stored in Google Firestore and protected by per-user security rules: an account can only read or write its own document - nobody else's, not even with technical tricks.
  • A copy is cached in your own browser (localStorage) so the app works offline and loads fast. Logging out clears it.

Third parties that see (only) your public handles

  • Google - authentication only.
  • Codeforces - official public API for contests and ratings.
  • Community mirrors (CompeteAPI, Contest Hive, alfa LeetCode API) and a purpose-built Cloudflare Worker (CodeChef history) - these receive the handle you asked to look up and return public contest/rating data. They never receive your email, password, or anything private.

Your control

  • Correct anything anytime in Profile (handles honor a 30-day change cooldown, shown under each field).
  • Log out anytime to wipe this browser's copy.
  • Ask for full deletion of your cloud data at any point by contacting via the LinkedIn profile shown in the footer, and it will be removed.

Changes

If this policy ever changes materially, the updated date above will change with it. Continued use of the app means you accept the current version.
